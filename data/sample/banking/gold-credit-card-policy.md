# Eligibility

The fictional Gold Credit Card is available to resident customers aged 21 through 65 who
have completed KYC. Salaried applicants need verified gross monthly income of at least INR
75,000. Self-employed applicants need annual taxable income of at least INR 900,000 and two
years of filed returns. All applicants remain subject to affordability, bureau, fraud, and
internal exposure checks.

# Comparison with Classic Card

The Classic Card requires gross monthly income of INR 35,000 for salaried applicants or
annual taxable income of INR 420,000 for self-employed applicants. It has a lower default
credit-limit band and does not include lounge access. Neither product is automatically
approved solely because the stated income threshold is met.

# Key fees

The annual Gold Card fee is INR 2,500 plus applicable taxes and is waived for the following
year when eligible retail spend reaches INR 400,000. Cash advances carry the fee shown in
the current Fees and Rates Schedule from the transaction date.
