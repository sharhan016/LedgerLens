# Savings products

Premium Savings pays a fictional annual rate of 4.10 percent on balances below INR 500,000
and 4.35 percent on the portion at or above that threshold. Its average-monthly-balance
shortfall fee is INR 350 plus applicable taxes. Standard Savings has no balance requirement
and pays 3.25 percent annually.

# Lending products

Personal Loan processing costs 1.5 percent of the sanctioned amount, with a minimum of INR
1,500 and maximum of INR 15,000, plus applicable taxes. Home Loan processing costs 0.50
percent of the sanctioned amount capped at INR 25,000, plus applicable taxes.

# Cards

Gold Credit Card annual membership costs INR 2,500 plus applicable taxes. Its cash-advance
fee is 2.5 percent of the amount or INR 500, whichever is greater. The schedule is synthetic
and does not describe any real bank's pricing.
