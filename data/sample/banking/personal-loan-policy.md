# Core eligibility

Applicants must be between 23 and 60 years old at application, have completed KYC, and
show a stable source of income. Salaried applicants need six months in their current role
and twelve months of total employment history. The minimum fictional net monthly income is
INR 35,000, subject to affordability and bureau review.

# Self-employed applicants

A self-employed customer may apply after the business has operated continuously for at
least two years. Required evidence includes the last two filed income-tax returns, six
months of primary business bank statements, business registration evidence, and the latest
profit-and-loss statement. Eligibility does not guarantee approval; underwriting also
assesses cash-flow stability, existing obligations, and verified business activity.

# Fees

The processing fee is 1.5 percent of the sanctioned amount, subject to a minimum of INR
1,500 and a maximum of INR 15,000, plus applicable taxes. No fee is collected before the
customer receives and accepts the sanction summary. The separate Fees and Rates Schedule
controls if it has a later effective date.
