# Purpose

This fictional Northstar Union Bank policy defines the Premium Savings Account for the
LedgerLens demonstration. It contains no real customer or institution data.

# Eligibility

Applicants must be at least 18 years old, complete standard KYC verification, and provide
a valid tax identifier. A minor may only be added through the separate guardian account
procedure. The product is available to resident individuals and sole proprietors; trusts
and incorporated businesses must use a current account product.

# Balance requirement

The account requires an average monthly balance of INR 25,000. The average is calculated
from end-of-day ledger balances for the calendar month. A shortfall fee of INR 350 plus
applicable taxes is charged when the average falls below the requirement. The fee is
waived for the first full calendar month after opening and whenever a service disruption
prevents normal account access for more than 24 consecutive hours.

# Interest and review

Interest is calculated daily and credited quarterly at the rate in the current Fees and
Rates Schedule. Product Operations reviews this policy every six months. When versions
conflict, the policy with the latest effective date controls.
