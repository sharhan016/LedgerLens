# Applicant assessment

Applicants must complete KYC and demonstrate income sufficient for the proposed repayment.
The fictional maximum loan-to-value ratio is 80 percent for properties valued up to INR 7.5
million and 75 percent above that amount. A lower ratio may apply after credit or property
risk review.

# Property evidence

The file must include the sale agreement, chain of title, approved building plan where
applicable, property-tax receipt, independent legal opinion, and approved valuation. Funds
are not released while a material title exception remains unresolved.

# Disbursement

Disbursement follows documented construction or purchase milestones and is made to the
verified seller or builder account. Exceptions require Credit Risk approval and an audit
note describing the reason and compensating control.
