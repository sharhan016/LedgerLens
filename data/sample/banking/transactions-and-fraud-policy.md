# Suspicious transaction handling

An alert must be reviewed against the customer's expected profile, recent activity, known
counterparties, device history, and relevant sanctions indicators. Staff must preserve the
evidence trail and must not tell the customer that an AML report may be considered. High-
risk alerts are escalated to Compliance; only Compliance can decide whether the fictional
regulatory report should be filed.

# Suspected account takeover

When evidence indicates account takeover, Digital Fraud may suspend digital access and
outgoing transfers while preserving inbound credits. Staff verify the customer through an
approved out-of-band channel, revoke active sessions, rotate credentials, and record every
restriction and release in the audit log. Emergency restrictions are reviewed within four
hours.

# Customer-reported card fraud

The agent blocks the affected card, records disputed transaction identifiers, explains the
temporary-credit timeline, and avoids promising reimbursement before investigation. A new
card is issued only after delivery details are independently confirmed.
